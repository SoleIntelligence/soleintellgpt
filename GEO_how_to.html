IF YOU RUN A PRACTICE

getting found by patients
  • Claim + FILL OUT your Google Business Profile. all of it.
  • Same name / address / phone EVERYWHERE. no typos.
  • Get real reviews — the AI reads the vibe, not just stars.
  • Write pages like patients TALK ("why does my big toe hurt??")
  • Get listed: APMA, Healthgrades, Zocdoc.

IF YOU ARE THE DOCTOR

being the one it quotes
  • ONE name. "Jane Doe, DPM." every single time.
  • Put your expertise where a machine can QUOTE it.
  • Get mentioned by sources AI trusts: journals, APMA, press.
  • One identity across every profile + your NPI record.
THE FINE PRINT THE HEADLINE SKIPPED

SEO isn't actually dead. For "podiatrist near me," the old playbook still does most of the heavy lifting — AI Overviews hit only ~7% of local searches. GEO is a NEW LAYER, not a teardown. And the punchline: the same boring foundation — complete listings, consistent facts, real reviews, clear answers — is what BOTH the ranked list and the robot's answer read. Build it once. Works on both.
