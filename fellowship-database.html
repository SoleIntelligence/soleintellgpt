How to read the tiers
CPME — the only tier with delegated accreditation authority (site visits, annual reporting, non-competes banned; no case minimums). ACFAS Recognized — College endorsement via self-attestation; no site visits or audits. Conditional — has not yet completed a fellowship year under Recognition. Research — non-surgical, research-focused Recognition track. Independent — no external oversight; verify case-logging rights for the ABFAS portfolio before valuing the credential.
