How to read the map
Marker area is the number of programs at a location, not a quality signal — a big pin is a dense city, nothing more. Hatched states have no program in the verified list; that is a real gap, not missing data. Tier tells you who audits the program: CPME-accredited is the only tier with delegated accreditation authority, ACFAS Recognized and Conditional are College listings via self-attestation, and Independent has no external oversight. Same five tiers as the database.
